Concept 03 · Modern editorial

Clear work, shared openly.

Open Grove uses bright space, precise typography, and quiet semantic color to make every tree-lot task feel direct. Information stays calm and readable; important actions remain unmistakable under pressure.

Design thesis

Open space. Decisive action.

Editorial rhythm, subtle rules, clear labels, and tabular numerals keep family workflows approachable while allowing Committee and Treasurer views to become genuinely dense.
